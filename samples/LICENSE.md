# Sample OPG Radiographs Attribution & License

The sample dental panoramic radiographs in this directory (`sample_opg_1.png`, `sample_opg_2.png`, `sample_opg_3.png`) are derived from the public **DENTEX (Dental Enumeration and Diagnosis on Panoramic X-rays)** challenge dataset.

- **De-identification:** All images are fully anonymized research dental panoramic radiographs stripped of Protected Health Information (PHI).
